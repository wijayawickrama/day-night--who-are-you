01 — HOW IT STARTED

01 / 03

A hobby that
found its rhythm.

I started DJing as a hobby in 2024, simply because I loved music and wanted to explore it in a new way. Learning to mix gave me a way to connect tracks, shape a mood, and tell a story over time.

Progressive house is my favourite genre. Its gradual changes and layered melodies leave space for a set to breathe, while each transition moves the energy forward.

02 — WHY PROGRESSIVE HOUSE

MY FAVOURITE SOUND

One groove.
Many directions.

Progressive house builds through layers, melody, and gradual changes instead of rushing to the next moment. I chose it because its steady groove gives me room to blend in sounds from other genres and let a set travel while still feeling connected.
